ECTO 007H — Engineering Diagnostics

Open index.html in Safari. F6 Engineering > SYSTEM DIAGNOSTICS.
RUN DIAGNOSTIC to scan, or inject a field/grid fault for testing.
ACKNOWLEDGE the alarm; choose CORRECT FAULT to open the diagnostic procedure.
IDENTIFY > set adjustment between 40 and 70 > APPLY > VERIFY.

Based on 007G.3. 007H keeps the established four-stage transfer logic and archive.
Data is stored locally under ecto_containment_007h, initially copied from 007G.
Keep a JSON export of your prior build before testing.
Thermal and auxiliary-power procedures are planned for 007H.1.
